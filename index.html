24 fruits, one book

Apple, strawberry, cherry, banana, watermelon, pineapple and many more. Each page shows the fruit’s name, so children learn the names of fruits while they play.

Easy tools for small hands

Paint freely with the brush, or tap an area to color it in one go. There’s an eraser for little mistakes, 4 brush sizes and 25 bright colors, and undo works as many times as you like.

Nothing gets lost

Every page saves itself, and the book shows each picture just as your child left it. Favorite pictures can be saved to Photos and shared with family.

Made for every screen

Fruit Book fits iPhone, iPad and both displays of iPhone Duo. Drawings stay sharp everywhere, and not a single stroke is lost when the device is turned or folded.
